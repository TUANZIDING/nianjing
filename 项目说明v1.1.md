# 念景 · Nianjing v0.2.0

[English overview](README.md) · [完整中文说明](文档/中文说明v1.1.md)

## 中文

定位：给普通人使用的诗意短片创作工具。一个想法开始，让摄影风格与纸上绘画相连，再根据自己的喜好组织动态和配乐。界面采用温暖纸感，支持心情、回忆、旅行愿望、幽默与轻讽刺。

本版增加完整中文 / 英文界面、三类指令输出、示例介绍、状态与错误提示、英文输入识别和英文修改。语言开关不重建表单、不改用户原话，也不重新请求生成。URL可指定语言，本地记住选择。

当前默认启动 `创作服务v1.1.mjs`；1.0文件作为旧版快照保留。配置结构、素材和本地作品沿用原版。规则导演仍不是语言模型，用户自己填写的原话和偏好不会被机器翻译。

无API时，使用熟悉的第三方创作并导入；配置自有官方API时可运行生成流程。项目没有收费 / 充值 / 会员窗口，候选平台不代表合作方。真实付费生成、账户权限和音乐质量尚未实测。

## English

Nianjing is a poetic short-film tool for everyday expression. Start with a thought; connect photographic imagery and drawing on paper, then shape the motion and music around your preferences. Its warm paper desk welcomes memories, travel wishes, humor and gentle satire.

This release adds Chinese / English interface copy, three creative prompts, example descriptions, status and error messages, English idea recognition and English revisions. Switching language preserves the form, original words and media, and submits no generation request. Explicit language URLs and locally remembered preferences are supported.

Start with `创作服务v1.1.mjs`. Files ending in 1.0 remain as the earlier snapshot. Provider settings, example assets and local projects retain their existing paths. The director remains rule-based; user-authored words and preferences are not machine-translated.

Create in an external service and import files, or configure your own official APIs. The project has no billing, recharge or membership UI. Listed providers are candidates, not claimed partners. Paid generation, account permissions and music quality have not been live-tested.
