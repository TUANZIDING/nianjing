# 念景双语创作指南 · Bilingual creative guide v1.1

## 中文总指令

```text
根据我的一句话完成10秒短片：先创作摄影风格与纸面绘画自然拼接的图片，再让它运动，配上符合我喜好的音乐。不要求我先提供照片。

我的想法：［自己的原话］
情绪：［可留空；允许幽默、轻讽刺和解压］
音乐：［喜欢的乐器、节奏和氛围；默认纯音乐］
画幅16:9，上半摄影、下半温暖手工纸上的钢笔与淡水彩，以不规则撕纸边缘相连。让一个故事物件跨过边缘并从真实材质变成绘画：海浪变水彩，倒影变河流，讲义变纸飞机。两种材质清楚不同，连接物件连贯，不加文字或水印。

视频保持原图构图与材质，让连接物件完成一个轻微、连续、合理的动作；克制运镜，不突然换景。音乐服从原话和我的偏好，与动作呼应，音量舒适、自然收尾。尽可能单独交付音乐，方便以后只换音乐。

交付拼接图片、动态视频、独立音乐（若支持）和有音轨的MP4。明确实际完成和未完成的环节，不把提示词或任务ID当作已经生成的作品。
```

## English master prompt

```text
Make a 10-second film from my sentence: first create a natural collage of photographic imagery and drawing on paper, animate it, and add music that follows my preferences. Do not require a reference photo.

My idea: [my own words]
Mood: [optional; humor, gentle satire and relief are welcome]
Music: [instruments, rhythm and atmosphere I like; instrumental by default]

Use a 16:9 frame, photography above and ink with light watercolor below on warm handmade paper. Join them at an irregular torn-paper edge. Let one meaningful object cross it and change materials: a wave becomes watercolor, a reflection becomes a drawn river, or lecture notes become a paper plane. Keep the materials visibly different and the connecting object continuous. Add no text or watermark.

In the film, preserve the collage composition and materials. Give the connecting object one small, continuous, believable action. Use restrained camera movement and no sudden scene changes. Let the music follow the original feeling and my preferences, respond naturally to the motion, and end gently at a comfortable volume. Deliver separate music where supported, so it can be replaced without changing the picture.

Deliver the collage image, motion video, separate music if supported, and a composed MP4 with an audio track. State what was actually completed and what remains. A prompt or task ID is not a generated work.
```

## 幽默案例 · My Mind Left Class Early

原话：一堂冗长的课还没结束，我的脑子先下课了。让讲义变成纸飞机，飞进纸上的彩色天空。

Idea: The lecture is still going, but my mind has left class early. Let my notes fold into paper planes and fly into a colorful drawn sky.

```text
Image: A16:9 collage. The upper half is a muted lecture room, notes on a desk and a slow clock. The lower half is a colorful watercolor sky on warm cream paper. One page crosses a torn-paper edge, folds into a plane, and becomes ink lines in the sky. Playful, gently satirical and freeing; no recognizable people, text or watermark.

Motion: Keep the collage composition. In the first 3 seconds, the clock moves slowly and the paper edge stirs. From 3–7 seconds, notes fold into a plane and cross into the watercolor sky. In the final 3 seconds, the plane traces a light arc and the picture settles. Subtle camera following; stable materials and no sudden cuts.

Music: A10-second instrumental. Begin with a repetitive piano figure, then open into playful piano and plucked strings as the plane lifts. Gentle humor rather than exaggerated comedy effects. No vocals, comfortable volume, and a soft ending.
```

修改 / Refinements：「音乐换成轻快古琴，不动画面」 / “Keep the picture; make the music light guqin.” 「换成左右拼接」 / “Use a side-by-side layout.”

此案例尚未生成成片；模型可能不遵循逐秒编排，生成后仍需检查。This example has not been generated. Models may not follow exact timing; check the materials, transition, motion and soundtrack after generation.
